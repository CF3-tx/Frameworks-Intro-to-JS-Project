Spring Equinox

Every year, on March 20 or 21, thousands of people gather at the Teotihuacán archaeological site to ring in the Spring Season. Dressed in white, with arms outstretched to the rising sun, particpants recharge their energy atop of the Pyramid of the Sun. The event combines ancient rituals with contemporary practices, and visitors come away with a new perspective on the deep past and the mysteries of today.

Mazatlán arrives to Punto México

Punto México: A space for exhibition and tourism promotion.

Discover the vibrant essence of Sinaloa at Punto México, a unique exhibition of gastronomy, crafts, culture, and tourism. Enjoy exquisite flavors, artisanal creations, and activities inspired by the famous Mazatlán Carnival, which captivated over 1.2 million visitors. Don’t miss the opportunity to experience an unparalleled tourist celebration that honors the history, talent, and warmth of a state full of beauty.

Ubicando en Av. Presidente Masaryk No. 172, esq. con Hegel, col. Bosques de Chapultepec, alcaldía Miguel Hidalgo, Ciudad de México, en un horario de lunes a sábado de 9:00 a 19:00 horas, vigente hasta el 30 de marzo.
